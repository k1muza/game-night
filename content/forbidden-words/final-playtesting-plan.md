# Final-pass playtesting plan (proposed, not conducted)

Use 4–6 player teams; recruit 24–48 consenting Zimbabweans aged 16–30, with appropriate consent for minors, urban/rural and both age bands, including Shona/Ndebele speakers. Counterbalance versions in 30–60s rounds. Capture target recognition before clues, correct/skip/wrong, seconds, forbidden violations, route, disputes, adjudication language, enjoyment and competitive fairness; compare conditional solve rates by cohort and version. Prioritise new African targets, hard familiar targets and flagged components. Repeat until each card has multiple independent observations, then revise and retest. Provisional flags: recognition below 80%, conditional solve below 35% or above 90%, repeated dispute or one dominant loophole. Do not label these empirical thresholds. Referees should prohibit actual direct translations and meaningful derivatives while allowing unrelated words; document disputed translations rather than guess. Native-language review remains required.
