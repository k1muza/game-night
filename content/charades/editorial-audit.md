## Review limitations and release decision

Single-editor candidate generation, canonical filtering, normalization, mapping and structural review only. Weighted scores use the requested dimensions but are heuristic estimates rather than independent per-card review. Mean 83.42%; 59 cards fail provisional thresholds. The 92% target is not achieved or certified. Recognition, regional balance (especially rural and Ndebele audiences), language accuracy, distinctiveness, family suitability and aliases need individual editorial verification and human testing. Some closely related franchises/characters have overlapping pathways, even when canonical prompts differ. Retain draft status; do not release.
