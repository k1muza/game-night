# Research evidence

- [The Play Manual: Charades rules](https://www.theplaymanual.com/party-games/charades/) and [Chardz rules](https://chardz.app/how-to-play-charades/) describe title/word guessing, silent gestures and category/word signals. These are rules references, not empirical familiarity studies.
- [Singing truth to power: youth existential struggles and popular music in Zimbabwe](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00083968.2024.2439477) discusses youth Zimdancehall in urban and rural areas. This supports including the genre, not every artist's individual recognisability or mime distinctiveness.
- [British Council: Seven Zimbabwean musicians](https://www.britishcouncil.org/voices-magazine/seven-zimbabwean-musicians-you-should-hear) provides context for Zimbabwean artists and Kure Kure/Mafaro.
- [The Herald: The false dichotomy between music genres](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/the-false-dichotomy-between-music-genres-in-zim/) describes Alick Macheso's Borrowdale/Kochekera dance routines and Zimdancehall artists.
- [UNESCO: Zimbabwe heritage list](https://ich.unesco.org/en/state/zimbabwe-ZW?info=elements-on-the-lists) documents mbira/sansi and Mbende Jerusarema. Supports conventional cultural concepts rather than invented situations.
- [The Herald: Which era had the best dramas?](https://www.heraldonline.co.zw/which-era-had-the-best-dramas/) documents Gringo, Studio 263 and older Zimbabwean dramas; not evidence of current 16–30 familiarity.
- Repository research.md: prior project strategy; not independently revalidated in full.

No comprehensive title/person source verification or dedicated current social-media audience research was completed. Individual sourceReferences are deliberately limited; most named references remain pending verification. Scripture locators are editorial references to verify against Bible text. No commercial deck copied. English and vernacular language accuracy requires native review.

House rules: no voice, humming, sounds, mouthing, spelling, writing, real props, external images or pointing to the answer. Category, word count/position, syllable and sounds-like hand signals allowed. Actors may mime at chest height or use hand figures; no physical contact, actual falls or dangerous movements. The category identifies intended answer type. Accept established aliases but not different entities.
