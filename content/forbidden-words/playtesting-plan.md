# Playtesting plan

Recruit 24–48 consenting young adults and youth (16–30), with appropriate guardian consent for minors, across urban and rural Zimbabwe; stratify 16–21 / 22–30, language and prior gameplay experience. Randomly assign baseline and revised cards to counterbalanced rounds, avoiding repeated exposure to the same target. In teams of 4–6 use identical 30–60-second turn durations. Record anonymous card ID, version, age band, language, pre-description recognition, correct/skip/incorrect, time, accidental forbidden clue, alternative route, dispute, enjoyment and fairness. Compare recognition, conditional solve rate, time, forbidden violations, skips, disputes and enjoyment; sample repeatedly until all cards have multiple observations. Initial flags (not empirically validated): recognition below 80%, conditional solve rate below 35% or above 90%, repeated disputes or single dominant loophole. Conduct native-speaker review of Shona and Ndebele translations and referee instructions. Repeat after changes. Do not collect unnecessary personal information. No test results yet.
