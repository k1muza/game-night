# Product vision

Game Night is the app a group opens when friends gather: fast to start, easy to understand, exciting to play. The app facilitates; the people are the entertainment.

## Principles
Speed · Simplicity · Social interaction · Legibility · Trust · Privacy · Entertainment · Consistency.

## Six product bets
1. **Quick Play works offline on one phone.** Hosting a multi-phone room is one tap further.
2. **One privacy ritual for every hidden card:** Pass → Hold to reveal (or tap twice) → 3·2·1.
3. **Tap rows, not swipe gestures.** Gestures and tilt are optional accelerators.
4. **The card is the hero:** a cream card on a dark table, readable at arm's length.
5. **Opponents referee.** The other team's phone is a monitor, buzzer and score witness, showing only what that role is allowed to see.
6. **Shapes before colours.** Every team, answer and state has a shape and a label.

## Direction
Card Table: playful, expressive, card-based. Ink #110E22 table, cream #FFF8EC cards, four game accents (Sprint #FFD23F, Charades #3EE0A8, Trivia #5B94FF, Forbidden #FF7A52). Typefaces are Bricolage Grotesque and Inter.
